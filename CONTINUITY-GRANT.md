# Opfield Product Continuity Grant 1.0

The Licensor, Alexandr Slavinschii, Republic of Moldova (alexandr.s@wiolett.net), gives this grant with every Opfield release. It cannot be revoked.

## Definitions

- The **license service** is the licensing service the Licensor operates for Opfield, at any address the Licensor publishes for it. It is available while any of those addresses answers.
- The **last release** is the last Opfield release published before the Continuity Event.

## When it applies

The grant takes effect at the first **Continuity Event**:

1. for 12 consecutive months, no new Opfield release is published and the license service is not available;
2. the Licensor publishes a notice that Opfield has reached its end of life and will no longer be provided; or
3. the Licensor dies or is legally declared unable to act.

Nothing else starts it.

## Source code under the MIT License

From the Continuity Event, the Licensor licenses the source code of the last release, including its commercial core, to everyone under the MIT License terms below. This license is irrevocable.

The Licensor, or whoever then holds the rights, publishes that source code, including the commercial core, in a public place within 90 days of the Continuity Event.

## Key holders

If you hold an Opfield license key that is valid at the moment of the Continuity Event, you also receive, for the plan and the installation that key covers:

- a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to keep using and self-hosting the last release, including its commercial core, as built and published; and
- the right to run that release without the license service: its license checks count as satisfied for your plan, and you may change that release, or use any offline activation the Licensor documents, so that it runs that way.

## What it does not cover

This grant gives no right to updates, support, trademarks, names or logos, and it does not cover third-party components, which keep their own licenses.
